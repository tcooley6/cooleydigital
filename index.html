FAQ

Questions, answered

Still wondering about something? Contact me anytime.

How long does it take?

Your free demo is usually ready within 3–5 days of filling out the form. Once you decide to move forward, we finalize the details and launch your site within 1–2 weeks, depending on how quickly we get your photos, logo, and info.

Do I own the site?

Yes. Your domain name and all your content (your photos, text, and logo) belong to you. If you ever decide to leave, we'll hand over your site files so you're never locked in.

What if I already have a website?

No problem. We'll look at what you have now, keep what's working, and build you a faster, more modern version. We can also move your existing domain over, so your web address stays the same.

What does the monthly fee cover?

The $50/month covers hosting, security, keeping your site online and running fast, and small updates like changing your hours, prices, services, or photos. You won't have to touch any tech yourself.

Can I make changes later?

Absolutely. Just text or email us what you want changed. Small updates are included in your monthly plan, and bigger additions (like a new page or online booking) we'll quote upfront before doing any work.
